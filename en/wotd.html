How Binance Word of the Day (WOTD) Works

Binance WOTD is a daily educational crypto word puzzle. You get 6 attempts to guess a blockchain or market term related to the weekly theme:

  • Green Tile: The letter is in the word and in the exact right spot.
  • Yellow Tile: The letter is in the word but in a different position.
  • Dark Grey Tile: The letter is not in the word at all.

Reward Eligibility Rules

Score at least 5 correct wins during the activity period to qualify for an equal share of the base reward pool. To unlock the bonus pool, complete the optional weekly trading milestone (typically $50 cumulative volume on Spot or Convert).
