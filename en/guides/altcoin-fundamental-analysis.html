How to Analyze Altcoins Before Buying: A Guide to Fundamental Analysis

Altcoins, or alternative cryptocurrencies, offer wide opportunities for investors. However, before buying any cryptocurrency, including altcoins, it's important to conduct a thorough analysis. In this guide, we will show you the steps required for fundamental analysis of altcoins. We will also emphasize the importance of using local models, such as the Qwen family via Ollama, for market analysis.

Step 1: Project and Team Research

The first step in altcoin analysis is to study the project and the team. This includes:

  • Understanding the project's goal: What exactly does the project plan to achieve? What problem does it solve?
  • Team information: Who are the founders and key team members? Do they have successful experience in similar projects?
  • Project history: Does the project have a history of success or failure? How long has it been developing?

Step 2: Market Research

Understanding the current market conditions is also critically important:

  • Altcoin market condition: What is the current market trend? Is there active trading?
  • Industry analysis: What technologies, tools, and approaches are being used in the industry?
  • Comparison with competitors: What other altcoins offer similar services? What are their strong points?

Step 3: Financial Analysis

This step includes:

  • Budget and finances: What resources have been invested in the project? Are there plans for using these resources?
  • Profitability and yield: What is the expected profitability of the project? Are there revenue models?
  • Capital structure: How is the project's capitalization distributed? Are there significant shareholders?

Step 4: Technical Analysis

In addition to fundamental analysis, technical analysis can be used:

  • Chart analysis: Study the historical charts of the altcoin. What is the trend? Are there crosses of key levels?
  • Trading volumes: What is the trading volume? High volumes can indicate significant interest in the project.
  • Indicators: What are the current values of key technical indicators? Are there buy or sell signals?

Step 5: Metrics and KPIs

The final step in analysis is to study key metrics and indicators:

  • Adoption rate: How quickly is the number of users growing?
  • Active users: How many active users are there?
  • Market share: What are the current and projected market share indicators?
  • Growth metrics: What are the current and projected growth indicators?

Importance of Using Local Models

Using local models, such as the Qwen family via Ollama, for market analysis has several advantages:

  • Speed: Local models can quickly process and analyze large volumes of data.
  • Adaptability: They can adapt to changes in market conditions.
  • Completeness of analysis: Local models can consider all aspects of the project and the market, leading to a more complete and accurate analysis.
  • Objectivity: The use of machine learning and artificial intelligence minimizes the human factor and reduces the risk of errors.

Conclusion

An analytical approach to choosing altcoins requires thorough and systematic analysis. A combination of fundamental and technical analysis, along with the use of powerful analytical tools such as local models, will help you make informed and well-researched investment decisions. Be careful and never invest more than you are willing to lose.
