Fundamentals of Technical Analysis: How to Read Cryptocurrency Charts and Find Entry Points

If you want to learn how to trade cryptocurrencies and make money, knowing Technical Analysis (TA) is a key skill. In this article, we will tell you about the basics of TA and show you how to read cryptocurrency charts and find optimal moments for buying and selling.

What is Technical Analysis?

Technical Analysis is a method of forecasting future market movements based on the study of historical cryptocurrency chart data. It includes a set of tools and techniques used to analyze the market and identify trends.

How to Read Cryptocurrency Charts

1. Key Chart Elements

To start analyzing cryptocurrency charts, it's important to understand the key elements:

  • Prices (High, Low, Close): The highest, lowest, and closing prices for a period.
  • Volume: The trading volume for a period.
  • Trends: Uptrends and downtrends, which show the price direction.

2. Main Chart Types

There are several types of charts used in cryptocurrency analysis:

  • Line Chart: Shows prices over time.
  • Candlestick Chart: Shows prices and volumes in the form of candlesticks.
  • Volume Chart: Shows trading volume.

3. Indicators and Signals

Indicators help to identify trends and predict price movements:

  • Fibonacci Retracements: Show levels where the price may retrace.
  • MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): Shows the difference between two exponential moving averages.
  • RSI (Relative Strength Index): Measures the strength of the current trend.

How to Find Entry Points

1. Identify Trends

First, find the main trend of the chart. It can be an uptrend or a downtrend. Choose the direction you plan to trade.

2. Use Indicators for Confirmation

Indicators will help you confirm your assumptions. For example, if the trend is an uptrend, and MACD triggers a signal line crossover, this could be a good buying opportunity.

3. Look for Crossovers

Crossovers of charts and indicators are important signals. For example, if the closing price chart crosses the MA charts, this could be a buy signal.

4. Ensure Protection

After identifying an entry point, don't forget about protective Stop-Losses. This will help limit losses in case of an unsuccessful forecast.

Using Local Models

It's important to remember that global market models may not always work for specific cryptocurrencies. For more accurate analysis, it is recommended to use local models, such as the Qwen family via Ollama. These models can help account for specific factors and trends in particular cryptocurrencies.

Conclusion

Technical Analysis is a powerful tool for trading cryptocurrencies. However, like any tool, it requires practice and understanding. Start with small trades and don't be afraid of mistakes. Over time, your skills and market understanding will improve.
