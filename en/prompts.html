📊 Fundamental Analysis

1. In-depth Altcoin Audit Before Purchase

Helps assess tokenomics, unlock risks, and the real value of a project before investing.

You are a professional crypto analyst and venture researcher. Conduct an objective fundamental analysis of the cryptocurrency project [COIN NAME / TICKER].

Break down the project strictly according to the following points:
1. The main problem the project solves and its real competitors.
2. Tokenomics: coin distribution (share of funds and team), vesting schedule, and inflation model.
3. Key growth catalysts in the next 6–12 months.
4. Main red flags and risks (regulatory, technical, risk of early investor sell-off).
5. Final verdict: is the asset suitable for a long-term portfolio or only for speculation? Answer concisely and without financial advice.

Here are the current token metrics:
- Pool liquidity and if it's locked (LP Lock): [INSERT DATA]
- Percentage of coins held by top 10 wallets: [INSERT DATA]
- Buy/Sell Taxes: [INSERT DATA]
- Is the contract source code open and are owner rights renounced: [INSERT DATA]

Assess the risk level from 1 to 10, point out hidden threats of price manipulation, and explain if it's safe to enter this asset with a small amount.
📈 Technical Analysis

3. Finding Entry Levels and Calculating Risk Management

Calculates a safe trade volume so that a single stop-loss doesn't significantly impact your deposit.

You are an experienced risk manager and algorithmic trader. Help calculate trade parameters and check its mathematical expectation.

My input data:
- Total trading deposit: $[DEPOSIT AMOUNT]
- Maximum allowable risk per trade: [E.G., 1.5]%
- Trading pair: [E.G., BTC/USDT]
- Planned entry price: $[ENTRY PRICE]
- Scenario cancellation price (Stop-Loss): $[STOP PRICE]
- Target profit-taking price (Take-Profit): $[TAKE PRICE]

Calculate:
1. Exact position size in dollars and in coins.
2. Risk/Reward ratio.
3. Is it worth entering this trade from a mathematical perspective (if R/R is less than 1:2, warn me about it).
🧠 Anti-FOMO Filter

4. Cold Shower Before an Impulsive Purchase

Ideal for local Ollama: ask this question when you feel like buying a coin on a green candle.

I am about to buy the coin [TICKER] because it has already risen by [PERCENTAGE]% in the last 24 hours, and everyone on social media is talking about an imminent pump. 

Act as an impartial risk manager and 'devil's advocate'. 
Provide 5 strong arguments why buying right now will likely lead to a loss or getting stuck in a position. Ask me 3 verification questions that I must answer 'Yes' to before clicking the 'Buy' button on the exchange.
