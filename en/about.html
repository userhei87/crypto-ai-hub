About Crypto AI Hub

Crypto AI Hub is an independent educational and technology portal focused on practical Artificial Intelligence applications in cryptocurrency markets and safe Web3 onboarding.

Our Mission

The crypto space is often clouded by complex jargon and hype-driven speculation. Our mission is to provide clear step-by-step tutorials, free client-side calculators, and tested prompts for local and cloud LLMs to help users build disciplined, data-driven workflows.

Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking

  • Hands-On Verification: All tutorials covering exchanges, self-custody wallets, local AI setups (Ollama, Qwen), and Python workflows are tested before publication.
  • AI Transparency: We use AI tools to assist with translation, structuring, and news summarization, while every published guide undergoes human editorial review.
  • Regular Updates: Core guides and weekly resources (such as Binance WOTD answers) are updated regularly to reflect current platform rules.
