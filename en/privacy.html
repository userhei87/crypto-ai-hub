Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crypto AI Hub (https://crypto-ai-hub.com) handles technical data when you browse our website.

1. Personal Data Collection

Crypto AI Hub is a static educational portal. We do not require user registration and never ask for seed phrases, private keys, wallet connections, or payment details. All calculators on this website run locally inside your browser.

2. Cookies and Third-Party Services

To display live market charts, analyze traffic, and serve advertisements, the website may rely on third-party providers that use cookies:

  • TradingView: embedded BTC/USDT price chart widget on the homepage.
  • Web Analytics (Google Analytics / Microsoft Clarity): aggregated, anonymized usage statistics to improve content quality and navigation.
  • Advertising Partners (including Google AdSense): third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

3. External & Affiliate Links

Our articles and tools contain links to external platforms (such as Binance and Telegram). Once you leave Crypto AI Hub, your interaction is governed by the privacy policy of the destination website.

4. Managing Cookies

You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, and you may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
